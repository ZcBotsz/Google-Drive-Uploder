Privacy Policy

How ZC Drive Upload handles your information and Google Drive access.

1. About this policy

ZC Drive Upload helps you prepare and transfer files from a browser, Telegram, or supported links to a Google Drive account you authorize. This policy describes how the service handles information when you use the Studio and optional Telegram bot. For questions or requests, contact @ZCQXx on Telegram.

2. Information handled

  • Google account and Drive information: Google account identifier, email, authorization tokens, the permissions you grant, available storage information, and file and folder details needed for your requested actions.
  • Files and transfer details: file contents, filenames, file sizes and types, captions, source links, selected destinations, transfer status, errors and Drive links. Browser files may be staged on the server for the transfer.
  • Telegram information: your Telegram user ID, name and username; relevant channel IDs and message references; and files or messages you submit or collect using the bot.
  • Service information: account settings, plan and usage records, session information, request and operational logs, and information needed to support or secure the service.

3. Google Drive access and use

After you authorize Google, the service uses the granted access to connect your account, show available Drive information, find or create destinations where permitted, and perform file transfers and other actions you request. The scope and visibility of Drive files depend on the permissions requested and granted. A narrower drive.file permission may not provide access to every existing file or folder.

Google authorization tokens are stored to maintain the connection; refresh tokens are encrypted by the application. Google user data is used to provide and support the features you request. ZC Drive Upload's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. Processing and sharing

Transfers may pass through the service's hosting infrastructure and configured Telegram Bot API server before reaching Google Drive. Google, Telegram, the hosting provider and other infrastructure providers process information as needed to provide their services. Service administrators may access operational information to operate, troubleshoot and protect the service.

Telegram logging and dump channels: if configured by the service operator, transfer metadata—including filenames, user and job IDs, Drive IDs or links—may be sent to a Telegram log channel. The service may also copy source Telegram messages or files and upload records to a configured dump channel. People with access to those channels can see the information shared there. The operator is responsible for keeping those channels restricted and configuring them in accordance with applicable consent and Google policies.

5. Storage, security and retention

Account, job, session and usage records are stored by the service. Files can be temporarily stored or streamed during transfer; staged drafts or files associated with interrupted or failed transfers may remain until removed. Successful browser uploads normally remove their server-side staged copy after processing. Retention also depends on operator configuration, backups and infrastructure. Security measures include authenticated sessions and encryption of stored Google refresh tokens, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and deletion

You can stop using the service and revoke its Google access through your Google Account connections. If you use the Telegram bot, /disconnect disconnects the Drive account from the service and attempts to revoke its Google authorization. Revocation does not automatically delete files already placed in your Google Drive or copies in Telegram channels.

To request access to or deletion of service-held account, job or staged-file data, contact @ZCQXx. The operator will need to verify the request and handle applicable operational, security, legal and backup requirements. You can manage or delete files in Google Drive and Telegram using those services.

7. Updates and contact

The operator may update this policy as the service or its practices change. The current version will be available on this page. Contact @ZCQXx with privacy questions.
